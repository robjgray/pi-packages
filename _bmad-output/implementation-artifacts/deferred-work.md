- source_spec: `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-pi-preface-situational-activation.md`
  summary: `matchReadToSkill` path matching has no normalization beyond `node:path.resolve` — symlinked skill dirs, case differences on case-insensitive filesystems (macOS default), and other non-canonical forms yield false negatives (a real `SKILL.md` read fails to flip the flag).
  evidence: Surfaced by the step-04 edge-case/blind-hunter review of the pi-preface situational-activation implementation. `conditions.ts:matchReadToSkill` does a raw `Set.has` against `resolve(ctx.cwd, path)` and the cached `filePath`; `resolve` does not resolve symlinks (`realpath`) or fold case. A future fix would canonicalize both sides (`fs.realpathSync`) and/or compare case-insensitively on case-insensitive platforms, with tests for the symlink and case-variant cases.
